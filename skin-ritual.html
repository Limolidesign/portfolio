Skin Ritual Aesthetic Co.

Summary

Client project
Designing a More Inclusive Skincare Brand

Skin Ritual is a clinical esthetics studio focused on personalized skincare, expert guidance, and long-term results. The project challenged industry norms by moving away from the hyper-feminine, wellness-driven aesthetics commonly found in skincare branding and replacing them with a modern, gender-neutral identity.

Discovery

Research

Challenge

Create an esthetics brand that feels modern, clinical, and welcoming without relying on the hyper-feminine visuals commonly found throughout the industry.

Goal

Create a distinctive logo system centered around an SR monogram that feels abstract, memorable, and ownable. The identity should also feel gender neutral, welcoming to every client without leaning on traditionally feminine cues.

Choosing A Direction

The final direction combines the clarity and trust of a clinical aesthetic with the personality and memorability of custom typography. Clean layouts, generous spacing, and a restrained color palette establish a sense of professionalism and expertise, while the custom wordmark introduces a unique visual signature that sets Skin Ritual apart from traditional esthetics brands.

Logo Ideation

Concepts

Inspired By Skin Itself

Rather than looking to existing skincare brands for inspiration, the identity was developed from the materials and experiences surrounding skincare itself. The fluid shapes of moisturizer and solutions across the skin informed the logo’s organic exploration, while other designs were based on sunlight passing over the body’s natural contours. The cream and the light moving over the collarbone were direct translations of how the logo should look and feel. These references became the foundation for a visual language that feels unique, intentional, and connected to the practice of skincare.

Choosing The Logo

Five logo concepts came out of the ideation round: a luxury monogram, a technical mono wordmark, a modular abstract mark, an organic symbol drawn from the way cream moves across skin, and a final direction that refined that organic idea into the custom monogram and wordmark. Option 5 was approved and carried into the full identity system.

Brand Identity

The system

Logo & Supporting Assets

The final identity system was designed to be flexible, recognizable, and consistent across every brand touchpoint.

Color and Type

Khaki Beige, Olive Moss, Alabaster Grey, White, and Black keep the palette quiet and natural, with the olive green carrying the logo so the work and the products do the talking. Cranio gives the wordmark its soft, flowing personality, while Antikor Text keeps labels, taglines, and body copy clean and clinical.

Mockups

Signage, stationery and merch

Structure & Contrast

Bold black headers, defined borders, and consistent layouts create a sense of order, professionalism, and neutrality. This contrast allows the softer, organic elements of the identity to stand out while reinforcing Skin Ritual’s position as a modern, gender-neutral skincare practice.
