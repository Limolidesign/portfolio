Summary

Personal project

LT Electric Inc. is a residential, commercial, and fire alarm electrical contractor. The old logo, a thin circle holding an LT monogram and a yellow bolt, was hard to read at a distance and hard to reproduce on gear. This redesign reduces it to one black lightning bolt and a heavy condensed wordmark on safety lime. It is built to be seen from the road, on hard hats and vests, vans, banners, yard signs, and shop signage.

About this project

A self-initiated concept, not client work. It starts from LT Electric’s existing logo and rebuilds the identity from scratch, then shows it on gear, vehicles and signage.
